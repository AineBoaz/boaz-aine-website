About

From laboratory science to computational discovery.

My laboratory background has shaped a practical, quality-conscious approach to scientific evidence.

Working with laboratory processes has shown me how specimen quality, standard procedures, documentation and quality assurance affect the reliability of results. Those principles remain important as I work with computational methods.

My current focus is developing skills in genomic analysis, statistical methods, R and Python, and reproducible computational workflows. My research interests include bacterial genomics, antimicrobial resistance and public-health data science.
