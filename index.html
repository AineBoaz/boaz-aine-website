01 / About

From laboratory science to computational discovery.

My experience in medical laboratory science—including laboratory quality systems and the principles of ISO 15189:2022—has shaped my commitment to reliable results, analytical accuracy and evidence-based interpretation.

Working across laboratory processes has given me an appreciation of how specimen quality, procedures, documentation and quality assurance influence the results on which clinical and research decisions depend. This experience continues to guide how I approach scientific data: carefully, critically and with attention to its biological context.

I am building on this foundation through bioinformatics and data science, developing practical skills in genomic analysis, statistical methods, R and Python, and reproducible computational workflows. My interests include bacterial genomics, antimicrobial resistance, pathogen evolution, infectious-disease research and public-health data science. I aim to contribute to African-led research, collaborate with scientists and institutions, and make computational methods more accessible through responsible consultancy, training and scientific communication.
